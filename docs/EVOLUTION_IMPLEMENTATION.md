# HodgeForm evolution implementation strategy

The product is a controlled transition system: exact artifacts and explicit change contracts compile obligations; verifiers try to falsify them; eligible evidence and an authorized human permit only that transition. ORB-World is a research fixture and prospective customer workload, not a prerequisite dependency or evidence of general intelligence.

## Delivery order and acceptance gates

1. **Obligation isolation (security fix).** Every evaluator must match the frozen requirement ID. Artifact-freeze evidence alone must leave capability inventory and regression missing. A later pass must not erase a failure. Use the same evaluator for UI and signing.
2. **Change intelligence.** Freeze content-addressed prompt, tool, policy, verifier, memory, world-model and representation components. Compare against the last admitted artifact on the server. Compile explicit obligations for modified/added/removed components. Declared contracts are not whole-program semantic proofs; independently produced scans remain a separate obligation.
3. **Evidence explanations.** Show per-obligation eligibility, identity, independence, excluded evidence, counterexamples and uncertainty. Bind every admitted result to artifact, policy, obligation and verifier execution.
4. **Discovery lifecycle.** Preserve proposals, competing hypotheses, scope, prospective protocol, evaluation criteria, data/holdout commitments and parent evidence. Freeze before evaluating. Append evaluations; any eligible refutation defeats survival. Inconclusive results stay inconclusive. Evolution candidates have no activation authority.
5. **Verifier bridge.** Admit only administrator-registered digest-pinned executables and public keys. Issue bounded, expiring, single-use jobs against exact candidate/obligation hashes. Receive signed results, reject replay, wrong subject, wrong key, wrong image, expiry and disabled identities. Execute each job in its own constrained container, without signing/API credentials inside it. Timeout and malformed output are inconclusive, never pass. Isolation and signatures establish provenance, not semantic truth or organizational independence.
6. **Deployment admission.** Verify pinned release keys, RELEASE, exact artifact/policy, target and validity window before a provider credential is available. Preserve single-use consumption and denial audit. Provider bypass must be closed operationally; a wrapper alone does not prevent deployment using another credential or auto-deploy path.

## Infrastructure boundary

The existing Railway app/database and $25/month total cap are the budget constraint. Do not add an always-on worker, expose Docker control to the app, or label a reused executor a disposable worker. A dedicated operator-controlled runner can process jobs on demand. Production worker acceptance and mandatory provider admission require real infrastructure tests and removal of direct bypass access, not merely passing unit tests.

## ORB-World publication intake (2026-09-06)

Input: `ORB_WORLD_PUBLICATION_PACKAGE_v0.1.zip`. Contains a preprint in PDF/DOCX/Markdown, bibliography, submission notes, figures, build script and publication index. It is a publication bundle, not the complete executable benchmark release.

Useful fixtures:

- A finite representation collision and one-bit repair: test whether a claimed representation extension separates a specified pair; preserve scope and distinguish constructed sufficiency from learning on untouched cases.
- Event skipping: freeze identity no-event dynamics and cross-entity event completeness; refute with autonomous drift or omitted effects. Counted operations are separate from wall time and energy.
- Four-channel localization: declare calibrated radial-law assumptions and synthetic data; physical transfer stays untested pending measurements.
- Muse/embodied control: preserve separate transport/graphics, EOG/EMG command detection, physical latency/accuracy and rendering claims. A working avatar does not authenticate sensor performance.

The preprint itself disclaims universal AI replacement, measured energy superiority, physical sensor validation and arbitrary thought decoding. Its Lean claim is a finite witness claim reported by the manuscript, not a fresh Lean replay performed by this intake. Preserve those boundaries. Do not revive withdrawn State-Inertia universal-memory, consciousness or novelty claims.

## Release discipline

Keep source/test/deployment evidence and remaining infrastructure gates in the handoff. Do not declare public GA, universal safety, a hallucination-free system or a new AI paradigm from bounded fixtures. The immediate commercial claim is preventing changes that have not satisfied an explicitly configured gate, when deployment actually requires that gate.
